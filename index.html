01 · Compatibility lab

Set your preferences. See the reasoning.

Every score is calculated from shared values, interests, intent, communication style, distance, and visible green flags. Change a checkbox and the ranking updates instantly.

60 km

Distance is illustrative for the demo. The app never asks for, reads, or stores your real location.

02 · Swipe deck

Like slowly. Match only when it is mutual.

This is a simulation. Some profiles are marked as interested in you so you can see mutual matches and starter lines. Nothing is sent to a server, and no real person sees your choices.
